Neon Jump Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Neon Jump, published by Neon Games One, handles information when you use the game.

Information We Collect

Neon Jump does not require users to create an account and does not directly collect names, postal addresses, contacts, health information, financial information, or precise location data.

The game may process or store certain gameplay information locally on your device, such as scores, settings, cosmetic selections, and purchase entitlement cache data.

Advertising

Neon Jump uses Google AdMob to display banner and rewarded advertisements. Google and its partners may process information such as device or advertising identifiers, diagnostics, ad interactions, and approximate location derived from network information, depending on your device, region, consent choices, and Google services configuration.

Where required, Neon Jump uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request and manage advertising consent and privacy choices.

In-App Purchases

Neon Jump uses Google Play Billing for optional in-app purchases. Purchase processing is handled by Google Play. Neon Jump receives information about purchase status and product entitlement as needed to provide purchased content. Payment card details are not collected or stored by Neon Jump.

Local Data

Gameplay preferences and progress-related information may be stored locally on your device. You can remove locally stored game data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app, subject to Android and Google Play behavior.

Data Sharing

Neon Jump does not sell personal data. Information may be processed by third-party service providers used by the game, including Google AdMob and Google Play, according to their own privacy policies and applicable law.

Children

Neon Jump is not primarily directed to children as defined by applicable laws. If this classification or the game's intended audience changes, the privacy and advertising configuration will be updated as required.

Security and Retention

We use reasonable technical measures to protect locally stored game information. Third-party providers may retain information according to their own retention policies. Local game data generally remains on the device until the user clears the app's data or uninstalls the app.

Your Choices

Depending on your region, you may be able to manage advertising consent and privacy options within the game or through your device and Google settings.
